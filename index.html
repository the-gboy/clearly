Structure is the protection.

Clearly doesn't guess. It queries. Six layers turn your messy reality into a single source of truth.

Your raw data is ingested and restructured for querying — not dumped in raw, but re-digested into a purpose-built database.

02

03

Relationship mapping

Targets, KPIs and business context are coded into the data relationships — not held in someone's chat memory.
